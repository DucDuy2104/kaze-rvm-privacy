Privacy Policy — Kaze - Routing Video Maker

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Kaze - Routing Video Maker ("the app") is made by DUC DUY. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

No account

The app has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

Your routes and photos stay on your phone

GPX files you import, the edits you make, the photos you attach to markers, your nickname and profile photo, and the videos you create are stored only on your device. We do not upload them. A video leaves your phone only when you save it to your gallery or share it yourself.

Map data

To draw the map, the app downloads map tiles from our tile server (tiles.kaze-vda.com, hosted on Cloudflare). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the server. Tile requests contain the map area, not your route file. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and your interactions with ads, to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy settings. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report through Google Firebase Crashlytics: the technical details of the crash, your device model and Android version, and an installation identifier. Crash reports do not contain your routes, photos or videos.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).
